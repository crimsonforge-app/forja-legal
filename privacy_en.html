Privacy Policy

Forja do Herói · last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what data the Forja do Herói app uses, why, for how long and how you can delete it (including under the GDPR and the Brazilian LGPD). Controller: [Vinícius da Cruz Pires], contact: [crimsonforge@gmail.com].

Data that stays on your device

Your hero, streak, coins and trigger and relapse records are saved on your device. They only leave it if you turn on cloud backup or copy the backup as text.

Cloud backup (optional)

If you turn it on, a copy of your hero is encrypted on your device with a 20-character code that only you receive, and only then sent to our server (Google Firebase). We cannot read it. If you lose the code, it cannot be recovered. You can delete the copy at any time in the app (Hero > Hero backup > Delete from cloud); it is also deleted when you choose Start over.

Tavern (chat)

When you post a message, everyone can see it for 24 hours: the text, the hero's name, class, title, look and, if you allow it, your streak days, plus the language tab (Portuguese or English). Each device gets an anonymous identifier (no name, email or phone number), used to limit abuse and to allow blocking and reports. Reports keep the reported message and the author's identifier for review. Chat suspensions keep the identifier and the end date.

Prayer to the Gods

The text you write, with the hero's name, class, level, days and app language, is sent to the app owner by email (Web3Forms service) and stored on our server. Do not write personal data in prayers.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store card details; we only receive the purchase confirmation.

Ads

The app shows Google AdMob rewarded videos when you choose to watch them. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID. When required, a consent form is shown on first use; you can change your choice in your Android settings.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data. To delete everything on the server, use the app (Delete from cloud; messages disappear on their own in 24h) or write to [crimsonforge@gmail.com] with the anonymous ID shown under Hero > Hero backup. See also Delete my data.

The app is a motivation tool and does not replace medical or psychological care. In a crisis, call 988 (US), Samaritans 116 123 (UK and Ireland), find a helpline at findahelpline.com, or call your local emergency number.

If this policy changes in a relevant way, we will tell you in the app.
