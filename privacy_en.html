Privacy Policy

Crimson Forge · last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains which data the Crimson Forge app uses, what for, for how long and how you can delete it, in line with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD). Responsible party: Vinícius da Cruz Pires. Contact: crinsomforge@gmail.com.

Data that stays only on your device

Your hero, day streak, coins, items, duties and quests, and trigger and relapse records are saved on the device itself. They only leave the device if you turn on the cloud backup or copy the text backup.

Anonymous identifier

For online features, each device receives an anonymous Google Firebase identifier (no name, email or phone). It is used to limit abuse and to allow blocking, reports, duels and invites. If you sign in with Google, this identifier becomes linked to your Google account (see below).

Cloud backup (optional)

If you turn it on, a copy of your hero is encrypted on your device with a 20-character code that only you receive, and only then sent to our server (Google Firebase). We cannot read its content. If you lose the code, it cannot be recovered. You can delete the copy at any time in the app (Hero > Hero backup > Delete from cloud); it is also deleted when you choose Start over.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google, Google Firebase receives your Google account's basic data (email, name and profile photo) to sign you in. The email is also saved on your device, only to show which account you are signed in with. On the server, we store your encrypted backup's code linked to that account, protected by access rules that only allow your own account. This is what lets you restore your hero on another device without typing the code. We do not use the email to send you messages and it is never shown to other players. You can sign out at any time in Hero > Hero backup > Sign out of Google.

Tavern (chat)

When you post a message, the following are visible to all players for 24 hours: the text, hero name, class, title, appearance and streak days. After that the message is deleted. Reports keep the reported message and the author's identifier for the moderator to review. Chat suspensions keep the identifier and the end date.

Duels (Player X Player)

When you challenge or accept a duel, the server stores both heroes' name, class, level and appearance, the coins in play, the points and the titles of the day's tasks, which the opponent can see. Duel records are deleted within 30 days after the duel ends.

Invites

If you join through a friend's invite, the server stores the inviter's anonymous identifier and the date, only to count their reward.

Prayer to the Gods

The text you write, together with the hero's name, class, level, days, language and the Special Request flag (for subscribers), is sent to the app's owner by email (Web3Forms service) and stored on our server for reference. Do not write personal data in prayers.

Reminders and notifications

Quest and duty reminders and the app's other notifications are scheduled on the device itself. The times you choose are not sent to the server. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

New version alert

To let you know when a new version is out, the app checks Google Play through Android itself. None of your data is sent to our server for this.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store card data; we only receive confirmation of the purchase or subscription.

Ads

The app shows Google AdMob rewarded videos when you choose to watch them. AdMob may use the device's advertising identifier. When required, a consent form is shown; you can change your choice in Android settings.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your real name, phone, location, contacts or photos. We only receive an email if you choose Sign in with Google.

Who the data is shared with

Only the services that make the app work: Google Firebase (server and Google sign-in), Google Play (payments), Google AdMob (ads) and Web3Forms (prayer emails). We do not sell data.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data. Use the app (Delete from cloud; messages disappear on their own after 24 hours) or write to crinsomforge@gmail.com with the anonymous ID shown in Hero > Hero backup (or, if you signed in with Google, the account email). See also the Delete my data page.

Minimum age

The app is not intended for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Teens aged 14 to 17 must use the app with permission from a parent or guardian, and their data is processed in their best interest. Parents and guardians can request deletion through the contact above.

Health and well-being

The app is a motivation tool and does not replace medical or psychological care. In a crisis, contact your local emergency services.

Changes

If this policy changes in a relevant way, we will announce it in the app.
