Delete my data

Crimson Forge · last updated: November 27, 2026

You can delete your Crimson Forge data at any time, in two ways.

1. In the app itself (right away)

  • Cloud backup: Hero > Hero backup > Delete from cloud. The encrypted copy is removed from the server.
  • Google account: Hero > Hero backup > Sign out of Google removes the account from this device. To also delete the account record on the server, send the request below.
  • Hero on the device: use Start over at the end of the Hero menu, or uninstall the app.
  • Tavern messages: disappear on their own after 24 hours.
  • Duels: records are deleted within 30 days after the duel ends.

2. By request

Email crinsomforge@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the anonymous ID shown in Hero > Hero backup (or, if you signed in with Google, the account email). We delete all data linked to that ID on the server (backup, Google sign-in, reports, suspensions, duels, invites and prayers) within 15 days and confirm by email.

Purchases and subscriptions

These are managed by your Google Play account. To cancel the subscription, use Google Play > Payments and subscriptions. Deleting the app's data does not cancel the subscription.
