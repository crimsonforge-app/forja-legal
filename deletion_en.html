Delete my data

Forja do Herói · last updated: [DATE]

You can delete your data in two ways:

1. In the app

Open the app, go to the Hero menu, scroll to Hero backup and tap Delete from cloud. The encrypted copy of your hero is removed from the server. To also delete the hero from your device, tap Start over (or uninstall the app). Tavern messages disappear on their own after 24 hours.

2. By request

Write to [crimsonforge@gmail.com] with the subject "Delete my data" and the anonymous ID shown under Hero > Hero backup. We delete the backup, messages, reports, prayers and suspensions linked to that ID within [DEADLINE, e.g. 30 days].

Purchases and subscriptions stay in your Google Play account, which manages them.
